# Support and feedback

Chora is pre-release software. There is not yet a formal support commitment,
but clear reports and workflow feedback are welcome.

## Questions and open-ended feedback

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/maykawacode/chora/discussions) for
questions, examples, teaching or research workflows, and ideas that need
conversation before they become a specific change.

## Bugs and scoped improvements

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/maykawacode/chora/issues) and select the
closest template. Include the Chora version or commit, macOS version, exact
steps, and the expected and observed results.

## Research data and privacy

Chora sessions and imported datasets may contain names, notes, judgments, or
other sensitive research material. Do not attach files or paste their contents
into a public Issue or Discussion unless you have permission to publish them.
Create a minimal synthetic example when possible.

## Security reports

Do not open a public Issue for a suspected security vulnerability. Until a
private reporting channel is enabled on the repository, provide only a minimal
notice through the repository owner's GitHub profile without including exploit
details or sensitive data.
