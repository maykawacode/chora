CHORA \ˈkȯr-ə\

For ideas, insights, and priorities

Decide what things mean. Change your mind as often as you like.

A free tool for exploring a list of ideas. Place each item between qualities you choose, such as simple and complex, and see how they relate on a map.

Start with three items and two qualities. Grow each study as large as you need. Each study runs locally in your browser. No account needed. Your work never leaves your computer.

Friction on purpose

Chora helps you get your head around a set of issues or ideas. It does not do the thinking for you. It adds friction where friction helps. You decide how items relate, one by one, and spend your effort only on the judgments that need it. That is where discernment counts.

How it works.

Position and reposition items

You can directly manipulate the position of an item or place it numerically via spreadsheet import. All views update with changes made anywhere.

Gather items into collections

Group items into collections, independently of their qualities and positions, to see broader patterns. Rename and redefine items, qualities, and collections at any time.

See the whole set at once

Semantic views place each item on every quality at once. Each item's line forms a profile you can compare with the others.

You decide. Chora keeps up.

Chora has no AI. Nothing is decided for you; every position on a map is a judgment you make. Changes are recorded in the study's Notes, so you can track how your understanding evolves. Save versions of a study and restore any of them later.

Yours, and private.

Chora runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to a server. It saves studies as files on your computer. Export the data to a spreadsheet, or any map as a slide-ready image.
